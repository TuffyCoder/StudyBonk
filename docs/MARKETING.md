# StudyBonk Marketing Kit 📣

The public, always-fresh version lives at **https://studybonk.vercel.app/marketing/**
(data source: `content/marketing.py`). This doc is the working playbook.

## Positioning

> StudyBonk is the 100% free, 100% private study platform with
> Duolingo-style gamification and a local AI tutor that never phones home.

One-liners by platform:
- **TikTok/Shorts**: "The study app that doesn't want your email."
- **Reddit**: "Free, no-account, open-source study tool — flashcards, quizzes, XP, local AI. Zero trackers. AMA."
- **YouTube**: "I built a study app with an AI that runs entirely in your browser."

## Content pillars (marketing, not topic pillars)

1. **Privacy contrast** — "every study app wants your email. this one wants nothing."
2. **Local AI wow** — "2-billion-parameter AI running in a browser tab, offline, no API."
3. **Gamification demos** — streak saves, level-ups, Bonk Challenges (screen record).
4. **Struggle→solution** — address a specific student pain per video (word problems panic, exam anxiety, 11pm cramming).
5. **Build-in-public** — "I generated a 75-page SEO site with Python" devlogs.

## Video formats that work

- 30–45 s hook→demo→CTA Shorts (full scripts on /marketing/)
- POV skits (ideas on /marketing/)
- Screen-recorded speed runs: "Speed Round in 47 seconds — can you beat it?"
- Before/after: messy notes → generated flashcard deck in 20 s

## CTA line

> Study free at **studybonk.vercel.app** — no sign-up, seriously.

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- Testimonials on the site are labeled as illustrative personas until real users exist.
- No fake user counts, no fake reviews, no engagement pods.
- Disclose creator identity on Reddit and in collaborations.
- Meme mode is a feature of the product, not the marketing strategy.
